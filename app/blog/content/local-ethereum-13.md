- **Vietnam:** Everything you need to know about the Vietnamese Ethereum ecosystem, [part 1](https://open.substack.com/pub/localethereum/p/vietnam-ethereum-ecosystem-overview-part-1?utm_campaign=post-expanded-share&utm_medium=web) and [part 2](https://open.substack.com/pub/localethereum/p/vietnam-ethereum-ecosystem-overview-part-2?utm_campaign=post-expanded-share&utm_medium=web).

## Global Ecosystem Update 🪐

### Scale L1, Scale Blobs and Improve UI/UX

- [Hyper-scaling state by creating new forms of state](https://ethresear.ch/t/hyper-scaling-state-by-creating-new-forms-of-state/24052). Vitalik noted that scaling Ethereum requires expanding three resources: execution, data, and state. The goal is to scale Ethereum by ~1000x over the next five years.
- Ethereum researchers [propose](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/hegota-headliner-proposal-focil-eip-7805/27604?utm_source=paragraph-email&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=post-delivery&utm_content=post-body) FOCIL as censorship-resistant headliner for Hegota upgrade.
- Carl [proposed](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/the-case-for-quick-slots-in-hegota/27708) "Quick Slots" for Hegota, which would reduce Ethereum's slot time from 12 seconds to as low as possible.
- Vitalik talked about [7 specific technical requirements](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2010621884811845708) that would allow the blockchain to ossify, with pre-emptive quantum-resistance cited as a non-negotiable priority for century-long security.
- Wonderland introduced [Interoperable Addresses](https://x.com/Wonderland/status/2018317430754344996?s=20) to make sending assets across any chain in the Ethereum ecosystem as simple as typing an address and clicking send.
- Ethereum Foundation [formed](https://x.com/drakefjustin/status/2014791629408784816) a new Post Quantum (PQ) team, led by the brilliant Thomas Coratger.
- [Native DVT for Ethereum staking](https://ethresear.ch/t/native-dvt-for-ethereum-staking/23894) by Vitalik.
- [ClientBeat](https://x.com/sina_mahmoodi/status/2013967198562205889?s=20): Ethereum Client Dashboard by Geth dev Sina Mahmoodi.

### DeFi, Stablecoins & Payments

- Y Combinator [opens](https://www.theblock.co/post/388230/y-combinator-stablecoin-funding) stablecoin funding option for startups starting Spring 2026.
- USDC payments [surge](https://x.com/circle/status/2017327081831727280?s=20) 70% as global finance goes always-on.
- Vitalik pointed out that [decentralized stablecoins face three unsolved problems:](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2010253778365055363?s=20) finding an index better than USD price to track, designing a decentralized oracle that can't be captured with large capital, and addressing the competition from staking yield.

### dDAI

- The recent [ERC-8004](https://www.8004.org) is live! It's a new standard developed by the Ethereum Foundation's dAI Team, MetaMask, Google, Coinbase, and others, that aims to enable AI agents to interact with various entities on Ethereum, allowing them to participate in a decentralized economy.
- [Welcome to ERC-8004 and the open agentic economy!](https://www.8004.org/blog/welcome-to-8004)
- [Clawd.atg](https://x.com/clawdbotatg): Built by Austin Griffith, this AI agent with a wallet builds onchain apps and improves the tools used to create them.
- Vitalik talks about "[Ethereum as an economic layer for AI-related interactions](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2020963864175657102?s=20)", which includes API calls, bots hiring bots, security deposits that potentially eventually more complicated contraptions like onchain dispute resolution, ERC-8004, AI reputation ideas.
